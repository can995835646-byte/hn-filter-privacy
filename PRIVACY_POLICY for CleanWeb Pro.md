# Privacy Policy — CleanWeb Pro

*Last updated: June 2026*

## What We Collect

**Nothing.** CleanWeb Pro does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to our servers.

## How Your Data Is Used

- **Page content** you choose to simplify is sent directly from your browser to your chosen AI provider (Anthropic, OpenAI, DeepSeek, or Google). We are not involved in this transmission.
- **API keys** are stored locally in your browser's storage (`chrome.storage.local`) and are never sent to us.
- **Usage counts** (free tier tracking) are stored locally in your browser only.
- **License keys** are verified once against Gumroad's API. We do not store or log your license key on any server we control.

## Third-Party Services

When you use CleanWeb Pro, page content is processed by your chosen AI provider:
- Anthropic (Claude): https://www.anthropic.com/privacy
- OpenAI: https://openai.com/policies/privacy-policy
- DeepSeek: https://www.deepseek.com/privacy
- Google (Gemini): https://policies.google.com/privacy
